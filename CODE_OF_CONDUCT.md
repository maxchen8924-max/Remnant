# Code of Conduct

Remnant is a project about sensitive memories, consent, and grief-adjacent
software. Contributors are expected to keep the work respectful, evidence-led,
and privacy-aware.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat contributors and users with respect.
- Handle examples and fixtures as sensitive by default.
- Discuss safety, consent, deletion, and provenance concerns seriously.
- Prefer clear technical disagreement over personal criticism.
- Avoid language or features that encourage emotional dependency on the system.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory conduct.
- Publishing private data, secrets, personal messages, or identifying details.
- Pressuring maintainers to weaken privacy, consent, audit, or safety behavior.
- Positioning the project as replacing, resurrecting, or simulating a person
  without clear evidence and consent boundaries.

## Reporting

For security-sensitive issues, follow `SECURITY.md`. For community conduct
issues, contact the maintainers through the project's preferred private channel
once one is published.
